## What you are looking at

Fifteen footprints marking where the sheets of the First Military Survey - the
Josephinische Landesaufnahme of Transylvania, 1769-1773 - fall across the
corpus's ground.

## How to read this layer

This is an **evidence** layer: it describes a survey, not a landscape. A
footprint tells you that a sheet of the survey covers this area, so that if you
want to know what the Habsburg surveyors recorded here, there is something to
look at.

Each footprint links to the repository that holds the sheet. **No scan is served
from this site.**

## Historical scope

1769 to 1773, the survey campaign for Transylvania.

## Sources and evidence

The sheets are held by the Kriegsarchiv in Vienna. The index of which sheet
covers what is reconstructed from published descriptions of the survey rather
than taken from a machine-readable archive index, because no such index was
available to this project.

## Reconstruction and uncertainty

**The footprints are reconstructed.** They are approximately where each sheet
falls, adequate for finding the right sheet and not adequate for georeferencing
anything. A footprint is not a georeference and this layer is not an overlay.

## Rights

No scan is redistributed. The rights in the sheets belong to the holding
institution, and the link is to the institution's own copy.

## Data fields

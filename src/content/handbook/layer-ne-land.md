## What you are looking at

Landmass polygons at 1:110 million from Natural Earth. An alternative to the coastline where a filled ground reads better than an outline, particularly under the territorial layers.

This record is deliberately short. A context layer takes the minimal-context
exemption: it owes a reader its source, its licence and any warning about how it
behaves over historical material, and it does not owe the reconstruction and
uncertainty sections that belong to a layer making a historical claim.

## What to watch for

The same caveat as the coastline: this is the modern shape of the land, not the shape it had. It is a frame, not evidence.

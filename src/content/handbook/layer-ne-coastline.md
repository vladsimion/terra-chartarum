## What you are looking at

Coastal outlines at 1:110 million from Natural Earth's public-domain physical vectors. It is the default base layer of this atlas: present on arrival, and making no claim about any period.

This record is deliberately short. A context layer takes the minimal-context
exemption: it owes a reader its source, its licence and any warning about how it
behaves over historical material, and it does not owe the reconstruction and
uncertainty sections that belong to a layer making a historical claim.

## What to watch for

A coastline is not a constant. Shorelines silt, deltas advance and harbours strand, and this layer shows none of that - it is the modern coast, offered as a stable frame. Where a historical layer places a port inland of this line, the port has not moved.

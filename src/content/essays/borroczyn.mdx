# The Parcel and the Bulldozer

## A city can be destroyed twice: once on the ground, and once by a comparison that hides how it was made.

The first release is deliberately smaller than Bucharest. It takes one bounded
Uranus–Antim seam across erased, relocated and surviving fabric. The rectangle
is an editorial research envelope, not a historical neighbourhood boundary and
not a cadastral parcel.

<Section id="seam" title="One seam, not a city" rooms={['city', 'map']}>

The selected envelope runs from 26.075° to 26.102° east and 44.417° to 44.437°
north. It is versioned as `borroczyn-seam-0.1`; expanding it requires a new
boundary and a new justification. Complete-city coverage is explicitly outside
this prototype.

The constraint matters because a bounded comparison can make its absences
legible. A city-wide mosaic would look authoritative before the seams between
source sheets, survey dates and rights states had been examined.

</Section>

<Section id="witnesses" title="Three evidence layers" rooms={['archive', 'city']}>

The interaction below is a provenance inspector, not yet the spatial reveal.
It keeps three things separate: the historical raster as received, geometry
derived after georeferencing, and the present OpenStreetMap reference. The
historical and derived states remain unavailable because their source cannot be
published and no pixel control points or residuals can be measured without it.

<BorroczynSeam />

Stereo 70 (`EPSG:3844`) is the working reference plane; the web map is a second,
explicit transform to `EPSG:3857`. A release needs at least six fit points, two
independent checks, and recorded RMSE and maximum residuals. Until then the
blank derived layer is evidence of work not done, not an invitation to infer a
fit.

</Section>

<Section id="objects" title="What the object can say" rooms={['archive', 'border']}>

Parcels, streets and buildings receive `urb-` identifiers in their own urban
authority. They may link to Corpus Nominum Daciae places; they do not become
places merely to borrow a stable ID. Every geometry keeps its source, evidence
layer, source feature reference, provenance and review state.

The companion In Manibus pipeline applies the same discipline to held maps. A
catalogue description or scan is not a physical inspection. No `obj-` record is
compiled until a named inspection has recorded recto and verso, state,
dimensions, folds or binding traces, colour, repairs or marks, and provenance
clues, and that inspection has been reviewed.

</Section>

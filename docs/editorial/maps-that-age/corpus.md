# Maps That Age - corpus and rights log

**Ticket:** KAN-114  
**Hero:** `age-ort1-1579`

The corpus treats an impression or documented state as an individual historical map
record. It stays within the three `Typus Orbis Terrarum` plates used in Ortelius’s
_Theatrum_, so differences can be attributed to a documented printing lineage rather
than to unrelated cartographic families.

## Evidence boundary

The corpus distinguishes three things that are often collapsed:

1. a scan’s catalogue date;
2. a specialist’s plate/state identification;
3. a visual feature observable in the scan.

Only the 1579 and 1587 public-domain reproductions are used in the hero comparison.
Rows backed only by a specialist state record remain catalogue evidence and do not
pretend to supply a verified image. Colour differences are excluded from the
state-change ledger because hand colouring may postdate the impression.

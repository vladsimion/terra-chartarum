## Scope and rights boundary

This is a Christian, medieval-European and Byzantine case study. The title names the
system under examination, not a claim that these nine objects exhaust religious
cartography. The Madaba reproduction selected here is public domain; a richer National
Library of Israel photograph with CC BY-SA licensing remains available if attribution
and share-alike treatment become useful. The Ebstorf original was destroyed in 1943,
so the corpus identifies its public-domain facsimile as a witness rather than an
original-object scan.

All nine source pages and rights statements live in the package manifest so the
editorial validator can enforce the hero count and minimum corpus size.

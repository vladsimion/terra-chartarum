## Hero

**Carta Pisana, c. 1275–1300** — the earliest surviving portolan chart, represented by
the BnF-sourced public-domain reproduction. It is the right hero because the essay’s
argument is visible before the reader knows any history: coast names and rhumb lines
make the sea dense while inland territory remains almost silent.

4. Andrea Bianco, portolan atlas, 1436 — Biblioteca Nazionale Marciana.
5. Petrus Roselli, Mediterranean portolan, 1466 — public-domain reproduction.
6. Fra Mauro, world map, 1459 — Biblioteca Nazionale Marciana.
7. Battista Agnese, portolan atlas, c. 1544 — Library of Congress.
8. Jacopo de’ Barbari, _View of Venice_, 1500 — Minneapolis Institute of Art.

These nine works exceed the eight-map exit criterion. The corpus favours
institutional scans and public-domain reproductions; no copyrighted scan is copied
into the repository. Remote reproductions remain tied to their source-page credit.

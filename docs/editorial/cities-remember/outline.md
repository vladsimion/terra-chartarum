# Cities Remember - outline and map assignment

**Ticket:** KAN-133

1. **The city survives as a fragment** - `city-forma-urbis`,
   `city-bufalini-1551`, `city-nolli-1748`. Begin with loss rather than a complete
   urban snapshot. A fragment ledger refuses to draw missing Forma Urbis slabs as
   known buildings.
2. **The city performs itself** - `city-schedel-nuremberg`,
   `trade-barbari-1500`, `city-civitates-1572`. Compare a monumental civic portrait
   with the repeatable city-atlas grammar through `CompareSlider`.
3. **Walls after walls** - `city-bufalini-1551`, `city-agas-london`,
   `city-turgot-paris`. Track how walls, gates and institutional compounds remain
   visible after their functions or fabric change.
4. **Public and private ground** - `city-nolli-1748`,
   `city-turgot-paris`. Use the required Allmaps-compatible georeferenced Nolli
   overlay with opacity control, control-point record and residual disclosure.
5. **Risk remembers the block** - `city-sanborn-buffalo`,
   `city-agas-london`. Contrast insurance-selected materials and uses with a civic
   view’s named places.
6. **An overlay is an argument** - `city-forma-urbis`,
   `city-nolli-1748`, `city-sanborn-buffalo`. Close by distinguishing genuine
   continuity, uncertain alignment and the later research questions each map makes
   possible.

Every section has a corpus assignment. The fragment ledger, city-view comparison and
georeferenced opacity overlay are the three planned interactive moments. All will
retain a prose/table equivalent and the overlay will expose its transform metadata.

## Method

“Memory” refers to information and urban orders preserved or reactivated through a
map, not to a city possessing a single collective mind. Each chapter names the map’s
original selection pressure: imperial architectural inventory, civic portrait,
atlas publication, topographic survey or insurance underwriting. Later reuse is a
second history.

The hero overlay will record control points, transform model, residuals, source
image, source date and modern basemap attribution. Opacity is a looking tool, not
proof that apparent continuity is exact.
